# Treaty engine

`ftep-policy` evaluates the versioned Accord independently from runtime compatibility. Inputs include acceptance, compliance state, scope, last session, requested schedule, and a real-life override. Outputs are explicit policy states and suggested scheduling—not destructive commands.

Compliance transitions are auditable. Suspension refuses future FTEP-authorized launches. Restoration re-enables eligible future launches. Existing games, saves, runtimes, local identities, sessions, and achievements are never deleted or modified as punishment.

Scope is explicit (`library`, `franchise`, or title) and expands only through a versioned policy decision. Hydration and other wellness reminders are advisory and can never determine entitlement eligibility.
